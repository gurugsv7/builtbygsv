Crawlable route links warning

Nine URLs were orphaned, including all four service details and five live case studies.

Fix: Every prerendered page now includes primary navigation. The project and service hubs also list their detail routes directly.

Branded error handling complete

The deployment returned a generic hosting 404 and the client-side router treated unknown paths as home.

Fix: Unknown client routes now show a branded page. The build emits 404.html with noindex, follow, which tells search engines not to list the error page. Verify the live HTTP status after deployment.

Metadata length info

The crawl found three descriptions over 160 characters and one title over 60 characters.

Fix: Homepage and location descriptions were tightened. Long project titles and descriptions were shortened without removing the useful subject.

Contact destination complete

The site had project buttons and email links but no indexable contact page that explained what to send.

Fix: /contact now offers email, phone, WhatsApp and a guided brief, while stating that the site stores no form data.

Limited recorded off-site data info
